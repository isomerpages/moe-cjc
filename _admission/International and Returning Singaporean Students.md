<h4><strong>Returning Singaporean Students</strong></h4>
<p>Returning Singaporeans seeking admission are advised to participate in
the SPERS-JC/MI which is a centralised placement exercise which typically
takes place in November each year for those who wish to join the Pre-University
1 level at the beginning of the next academic year.</p>
<p>Please visit MOE website <a href="https://www.moe.gov.sg/returning-singaporeans/post-secondary/spers" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">https://www.moe.gov.sg/returning-singaporeans/post-secondary/spers</a> to
find out more.</p>
<p>Returning Singaporeans who have done the School Placement Exercise for
Returning Singaporeans (SPERS-JC/MI) test and have <strong>Catholic Junior College</strong> on
their results list may approach us for admission application.</p>
<p>You may submit your applications via this link <a href="http://apply.catholicjc.edu.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">apply.catholicjc.edu.sg</a>
</p>
<h4><strong>International Students</strong></h4>
<p>International students seeking admission are to apply directly to the
college.&nbsp; As the examination system differs from country to country,
we will consider such applications on a case-by-case basis.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Students are strongly encouraged to submit J-PACT results for the college’s
consideration. If found suitable, the student may undergo an interview
process and take school-based placement tests.</p>
<p>We will consider the application for admission of international students
for 2027 JC1 admission AFTER the release of school posting of Joint Admission
Exercise. Applications by international students are evaluated based on
merit and are subject to the international students meeting the college’s
admission criteria and availability of vacancies.</p>
<p>Applicants are to submit their application via the link <a href="http://apply.catholicjc.edu.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">apply.catholicjc.edu.sg</a> by
31 December of each year for admission in the following year, and they
will be contacted only if shortlisted.</p>
