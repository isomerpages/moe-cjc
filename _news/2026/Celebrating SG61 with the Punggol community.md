<p>Catholic Junior College staff and students joined residents from Punggol
in a qigong exercise while celebrating Singapore's 61st National Day on
the morning of 7 August, as part of the college's National Day commemoration
and outreach to the Punggol community.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/National_Day_at_Punggol_d_website.jpg">
</div>
<p>We were privileged to welcome Deputy Prime Minister (DPM), Minister for
Trade and Industry (Trade) and Grassroots Adviser, Punggol North, Mr Gan
Kim Yong as our Guest of Honour, who delivered inspiring opening remarks
about our shared future. DPM Gan welcomed CJC and said that he “looked
forward to the CJC’s community activities and engagement in the Punggol
district.”</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/National_Day_at_Punggol_b.jpg">
</div>
<p>Mr Bernard Tan, Chairman of CJC’s School Management Committee, also announced
the launch of CJC’s fundraising campaign for the new campus. He said he
“hopes that CJC alumni, parents, friends and partners will join us in generously
contributing to building a vibrant campus to serve all Singaporeans.”</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/National_Day_at_Punggol_r.jpg">
</div>
<p>Officiated by our Principal, Mrs Woo Soo Min, this year’s celebration
was an unforgettable one as CJC enjoyed a morning qigong session led by
more than a hundred local Punggol seniors from the Qigong Shi Ba Shi Association.&nbsp;
Mrs Woo shared that she looks forward to CJC being more involved and integrated
with the community in Punggol.&nbsp;</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 100%" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/National_Day_at_Punggol_i.jpg">
</div>
<p>The celebrations concluded with a vibrant and energetic singalong, featuring
National Day hits both new and old, creating anticipation for the warmth
and camaraderie that our CJC family will bring to our new community. Here’s
to our new beginning in Punggol!&nbsp;</p>
